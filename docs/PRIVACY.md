# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 3, 2026**

Unmark is a macOS menu bar utility that converts Markdown text into rich text (RTF), HTML, or plain text on your clipboard. This privacy policy explains how Unmark handles your data.

## TL;DR

Unmark does not collect, store, transmit, or share any user data. Everything runs locally on your Mac. Your clipboard contents never leave your device.

## What we collect

**Nothing.**

Unmark has no servers, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no accounts, no logins, and no network communication of any kind.

## What we access on your Mac

| Data | How it is used | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Text you type or paste into Unmark | Converted locally to your chosen output format | Your system clipboard only |
| System clipboard (read/write) | Unmark writes the converted output to the clipboard so you can paste it into other apps | Stays on your Mac |
| Your "Launch at Login" preference | Stored in macOS user defaults if you enable it | Stays on your Mac |
| Your selected output format (Rich Text / HTML / Plain Text) | Stored in macOS user defaults so it remembers your choice | Stays on your Mac |

## What we do NOT do

- We do not transmit any data over the internet.
- We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII).
- We do not use third-party SDKs that collect data (no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting services).
- We do not share, sell, or rent any data — because we don't have any.
- We do not retain your input text after the auto-clear timer or your manual clear.
- We do not access files outside the application sandbox.
- We do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, photos, or any other personal data sources.

## App Sandbox

Unmark runs inside Apple's App Sandbox, which restricts the app's access to your Mac at the operating system level. The app has no network entitlement and cannot make outbound connections.

## Third-party software

Unmark uses the open-source [Down](https://github.com/johnxnguyen/Down) library to parse Markdown. Down runs entirely locally on your device and does not transmit any data.

## Children's privacy

Unmark does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If we ever change what Unmark does in a way that affects this policy, we will update this document and the "Last updated" date above.
