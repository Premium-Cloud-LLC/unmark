## Description (max 4000 characters)

```
Unmark is the fastest way to turn raw Markdown into beautifully formatted text on your Mac.

Paste any Markdown — from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, your notes app, or a documentation snippet — and Unmark instantly converts it to rich text on your clipboard. Switch to your email, document, or chat app and paste. The formatting comes through.

No dialogs. No buttons. No friction.

— HOW IT WORKS —

1. Click the Unmark icon in your menu bar.
2. Paste Markdown into the box.
3. Done. The formatted version is already on your clipboard.

Switch to Mail, Pages, Notes, Word, Slack, Notion, or anywhere else, and paste. Headings stay headings. Bold stays bold. Lists stay lists. Code blocks keep their monospace formatting.

— BUILT FOR THE AI CHATBOT WORKFLOW —

If you spend time copying Markdown out of ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini and pasting it into apps that don't understand Markdown syntax — Unmark is for you. No more asterisks and pound signs littered through your emails and documents.

— FEATURES —

• Lives in your menu bar, out of your way
• Real-time conversion as you paste or type
• Auto-copies to your clipboard the moment conversion happens
• Three output formats: Rich Text, HTML, or Plain Text
• Auto-clears after 5 seconds so it's ready for the next paste
• Manual Clear button when you want it gone faster
• Launch at login (optional)
• Visual confirmation when copy completes — no annoying notifications

— SUPPORTED MARKDOWN —

• Headings (h1–h6)
• Bold, italic, bold-italic
• Inline code and fenced code blocks
• Bullet lists and numbered lists (including nested)
• Links
• Tables
• Blockquotes
• Horizontal rules
• Everything in CommonMark and most GFM extensions

— PRIVACY FIRST —

Unmark does not collect any data. Ever. There are no servers, no analytics, no accounts, and no network access. Your clipboard contents never leave your Mac. The app runs in Apple's App Sandbox with no network entitlement at all.

— SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS —

macOS 13 Ventura or later. Apple Silicon and Intel Macs supported.
```
